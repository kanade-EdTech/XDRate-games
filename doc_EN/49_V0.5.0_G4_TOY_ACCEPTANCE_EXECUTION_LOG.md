- The first screen shows `XDRATE GAMES` and game-rating content only, with no music entry shell;
- Exported game cards use `XDRATE GAMES · RATING CARD` in the header instead of the music-card label;
- Four covered personal-rating templates appear as a horizontal icon strip; details open only after clicking an icon. Black Myth: Wukong is selected by default, followed by Genshin, Call of Duty 4, and Red Alert 2;
- The four templates can be applied, copied, renamed, and deleted. Existing local templates only receive missing platform/version/studio metadata; existing scores and text are preserved;
- Built-in metadata labels follow the domain wording: “制作公司” for Black Myth: Wukong, “厂商” for Genshin Impact, and the default “工作室” for Call of Duty 4 and Red Alert 2;
- The radar chart now supports up to twelve axes, matching the game-template contract; all nine Black Myth: Wukong axes are rendered instead of being cut off by the previous eight-axis cap;
- Game name, optional platform/device, optional version/release year, editable release-information label (default “Studio”), release value, axes, reasons, deductions, and optional signature are editable;
- Generate-and-save PNG and Share rating card actions are visible;
- After content changes, sharing offers “Generate new image / Cancel / Share old image,” with Cancel in the middle;
- Real album and system-share availability remains dependent on host permissions and device capabilities.
